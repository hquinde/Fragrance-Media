## What works (from Winners)

- Curiosity + mild transgression ("feels illegal," "secrets mechanics know")
- Financial consequence framing — tie behavior to money
- Contrarian/counter-intuitive titles stop the scroll
- Ranked and comparative formats perform reliably
- Specific numbers in the title outperform vague claims
- Brand/marque-specific titles drive enthusiast engagement
- Aspirational + accessible framing taps middle-market audience
- Short punchy subtitle text on the title slide reinforces the hook

## Audience scope

TrackAI works with any vehicle that has a VIN.
Mix vehicle types across a batch: Cars, Trucks, Motorcycles, RVs, ATVs.
Never write "vehicle" — always name the specific type.
