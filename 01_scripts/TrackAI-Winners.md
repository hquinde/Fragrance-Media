## Patterns That Work

- **Curiosity + mild transgression** ("feels illegal," "secrets mechanics know") drives high clicks.
- **Financial consequence framing** lands consistently — tie car behavior to money.
- **Contrarian/counter-intuitive titles** ("Speed doesn't come from going faster") stop the scroll.
- **Ranked and comparative formats** ("most reliable," "worst roads," "Top 5") perform reliably — the external account's top 3 are all rankings.
- **Specific numbers in the title** outperform vague claims every time.
- **Brand/marque-specific titles** (BMW, M lite) drive outsized engagement from enthusiast audiences — specificity signals credibility.
- **Aspirational + accessible framing** ("fast cars under 10k," "sporty daily drivers") taps a massive middle-market audience.
- **Short, punchy subtitle text** on the title slide reinforces the hook — one line, no explanation.
