## Patterns That Work

- "Only X you need" framing — implies authority and saves
- Numbered lists (5 fragrances) — clear value, easy to follow
- Brand-specific dupe editions — high intent audience, searchable
- Dupe content for iconic luxury fragrances (e.g. BR540) — high search intent, broad appeal
- "Always get compliments" angle — social proof framing, highly shareable
- Aspiration framing ("smell expensive") — top performer, broad appeal
- Seasonal rotation format — timely and practical
- Curiosity hook ("Everyone will ask…") — drives engagement and shares
